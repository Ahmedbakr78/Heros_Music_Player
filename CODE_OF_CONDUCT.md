# Code of Conduct

## Be kind

Heros Player is built by humans, for humans who love music. Be respectful in
issues, PRs, and discussions. No harassment, no hate, no spam, no demands.

## Scope

Applies everywhere in this repo and its community spaces. Maintainers may
remove contributions that violate it and, for repeat behavior, restrict
participation.

## Enforcement

Report privately via the contact in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) (it reaches
the maintainer without a public thread). All reports stay confidential.
