# Heros Player — Full YouTube Music Remote

**Detect everything. Control everything. Panel-only. 100% local. Free and unlimited.**

Open YouTube Music, press play — the side panel becomes a full remote in
about a second: true song readout with cover art, play/pause, next/previous
that keep your queue alive, live seek, remote volume, shuffle, repeat, likes.

## Why Heros Player?

| Instead of… | You get… |
|---|---|
| Alt-tabbing to pause | One `Space` in the panel |
| A dumb mini-player | True title/artist/cover + live seek |
| Broken queues | Real site buttons — radio keeps flowing |
| Paywalled "pro remotes" | Everything free, unlimited, MIT |
