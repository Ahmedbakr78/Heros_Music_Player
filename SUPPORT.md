# Support — where to get help

1. **Start here**: [FAQ (25 questions)](./README.md#faq--25-questions) and the
   [User Guide](./docs/USER_GUIDE.md) — most answers are one scroll away.
2. **Bug?** [Report it](https://github.com/Ahmedbakr78/Heros_Music_Player/issues/new/choose)
   with the bug template (version, browser, repro steps).
3. **Idea?** [Request it](https://github.com/Ahmedbakr78/Heros_Music_Player/issues/new/choose)
   — one idea per issue.
4. **Security?** Private channel only — see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

Response time: best effort, usually within a few days. Response quality:
much better with screenshots and exact versions.
